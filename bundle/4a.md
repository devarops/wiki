title: Authorship-Preserving AI Assistance
description: A guideline bounding AI to structure, questions, and deterministic edits, keeping prose authorship human.
resource: https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/
tags: [llm-agents, accountability, writing]
---

AI assistance erodes authorship when the AI writes prose the author would otherwise have written.
The AI is bounded to structure, question, and deterministic edits; the author writes every word that involves choice.
The ICMJE recommendations make drafting or critical intellectual review one of the gates for authorship.
When the AI drafts and the author only approves, the contribution becomes writing assistance, which journals acknowledge but do not credit as authorship.
Approving the AI's phrasing is ratification, not authorship.
AI can never be accountable for the work, so it can never be an author.
The AI still helps through structure, questions, and deterministic edits — the work that involves no choice of wording.
